# Wikipedia Publishing Guide — SmartGarbage Chintalavalasa

## ⚠️ Before You Start: Notability Requirements

Wikipedia requires that subjects have **significant coverage in reliable, independent sources**. This means:

### You NEED (before submitting):

1. **At least 2–3 independent secondary sources** — NOT the website itself
   - News articles from Indian publications (The Hindu, Deccan Chronicle, Times of India, etc.)
   - Government reports mentioning the portal (SBM reports, Andhra Pradesh state reports)
   - Academic papers about digital waste management in India
   - Independent technology reviews or blog posts

2. **No conflict of interest** — If you are affiliated with the project (you are), you must:
   - Disclose your conflict of interest on your Wikipedia user page
   - Request edits through Wikipedia's conflict-of-interest noticeboard
   - Let independent editors review and improve the article
   - See: [Wikipedia:Conflict of interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest)

### You SHOULD have:

3. **A notable operator** — The Chintalavalasa Gram Panchayat should already have its own Wikipedia article or be mentioned in one
4. **External verification** — The portal should be referenced in at least one government publication
5. **Media coverage** — At least one newspaper or magazine has written about the portal

## Step-by-Step Publishing Process

### Step 1: Gather Sources (1–2 weeks)

Before writing anything, collect independent sources:

| Source Type | Where to Look | Example |
|---|---|---|
| News articles | Google News, The Hindu, Deccan Chronicle | "Chintalavalasa deploys digital waste portal" |
| Government reports | SBM website, AP state portal | Annual report mentioning SmartGarbage |
| Academic papers | Google Scholar, ResearchGate | "Digital governance in Andhra Pradesh panchayats" |
| Tech blogs | Medium, Dev.to, YourStory | "Building SmartGarbage: a Flask-based waste portal" |

**Tip:** If no independent sources exist yet, you can:
- Contact local journalists and pitch the story
- Write a case study and submit to a government publication
- Present at a conference and get academic coverage
- Publish a blog post on an independent platform

### Step 2: Create a Wikipedia Account

1. Go to [en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org)
2. Click "Create account" in the top right
3. Choose a username (avoid promotional names — use something neutral)
4. Complete the email verification

### Step 3: Disclose Your Conflict of Interest

1. Go to your user page: `en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:YourUsername`
2. Add this text:

```
== Conflict of Interest ==
I am affiliated with the SmartGarbage Chintalavalasa project and have a
[[WP:COI|conflict of interest]] in editing articles about it. I will use
[[WP:COI|appropriate channels]] for content contributions.
```

### Step 4: Create a Draft (Recommended)

Instead of creating an article directly (which may be speedily deleted), submit through Articles for Creation:

1. Go to [Wikipedia:Articles for creation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_creation)
2. Click "Draft an article"
3. Paste the content from `smartgarbage-stub.md` (without the HTML comments)
4. Add your sources in the References section
5. Submit for review

### Step 5: Wait for Review (1–4 weeks)

Wikipedia volunteer reviewers will:
- Check if the article meets notability requirements
- Verify all claims are sourced
- Ensure neutral tone (no promotional language)
- Suggest improvements

### Step 6: Respond to Feedback

If reviewers request changes:
- Make the requested edits to your draft
- Add more sources if needed
- Remove any promotional language
- Resubmit for review

---

## Common Reasons for Rejection

| Reason | How to Fix |
|---|---|
| **Not notable** | Gather more independent sources |
| **Promotional tone** | Rewrite in neutral, encyclopedic language |
| **Primary sources only** | Add secondary sources (news, reports, papers) |
| **Conflict of interest** | Disclose COI, let independent editors review |
| **Original research** | Only include verifiable facts from reliable sources |
| **No independent coverage** | Get news coverage first, then write the article |

## Alternative: Wikidata Entry

If Wikipedia notability is hard to meet, create a **Wikidata item** instead:

1. Go to [wikidata.org](https://www.wikidata.org)
2. Click "Create a new item"
3. Fill in:
   - **Label:** SmartGarbage Chintalavalasa
   - **Description:** Digital waste management portal in Andhra Pradesh, India
   - **Instance of:** website, software
   - **Inception:** 2026
   - **Operating organization:** Chintalavalasa Gram Panchayat
   - **Official website:** https://smartgarbage.onrender.com
   - **Country:** India
   - **State:** Andhra Pradesh
   - **District:** Vizianagaram

Wikidata items are easier to create and still provide entity recognition for AI systems like ChatGPT and Google Knowledge Graph.
