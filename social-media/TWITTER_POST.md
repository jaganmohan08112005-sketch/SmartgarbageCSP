🧵 Thread: How I built SmartGarbage

1/ The problem: Citizens in rural India don't know when garbage is collected, can't report missed pickups, and have no visibility into waste management.

- Live transparency dashboards
- Green Points incentives

3/ Tech stack:
- Backend: Python/Flask
