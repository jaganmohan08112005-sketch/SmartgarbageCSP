Enter your tracking link to see the real-time status of your complaint.

📋 How to Track

When you file a complaint, you receive a tracking link via SMS or on the confirmation page. This link looks like:

https://smartgarbage.onrender.com/track/abc123...xyz

Click the link or paste it into your browser to see:

  • Current status (Submitted → Under Review → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved)
  • SLA deadline and expected resolution time
  • Before/after photos (once resolved)
  • Option to reopen if the issue persists

📞 Lost Your Link?

If you've lost your tracking link, you can:

  • Check your SMS inbox for the tracking message
  • Contact the helpline: 1800-119-9111
  • Visit the Gram Panchayat office with your complaint details
