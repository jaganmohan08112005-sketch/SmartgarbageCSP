How to raise a grievance

  1. Step 1 — the complaint itself. File or track a report on the Report an Issue page and use the Track page to follow it. Every complaint carries a 48-hour resolution target for its ward.
  2. Step 2 — reopen it. If it is marked resolved but the problem remains, use the Reopen button on your tracking page. That puts the complaint back with the sanitation supervisor and records your dissatisfaction.
  3. Step 3 — escalate to the officer above. Call or email the Grievance Redressal Officer with the complaint reference number shown on your tracking page. Please include the ward number and the date you reported it.
  4. Step 4 — Gram Sabha. Unresolved grievances are placed before the Gram Sabha for review at its next scheduled meeting.

Response timelines

Stage Target
Ward sanitation staff resolve a filed complaint 48 hours
Supervisor reviews a reopened complaint 48 hours
Acknowledgement of a grievance sent to the officer 7 days
Final reply, or the reason it cannot be resolved 30 days

These targets follow the citizen-charter expectations set out in the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW).
