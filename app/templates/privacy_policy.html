1. {{ _('What We Collect') }}

a) {{ _('Information you give us in forms') }}

  • {{ _('Account registration:') }} {{ _('your name, a phone number (validated as an Indian mobile number), role, and optional email. Passwords are stored only as scrambled hashes.') }}
  • {{ _('Missed-pickup & issue reports:') }} {{ _('your name, phone number, ward/area, street address, issue description, and the date/time of the missed collection.') }}
  • {{ _('Waste declarations:') }} {{ _('the weight of wet, dry, sanitary and hazardous waste you declare, the ward, and a timestamp — used for segregation statistics and Green Points.') }}
  • {{ _('Illegal-dump reports (anonymous):') }} {{ _('category, description, ward and location — deliberately stored without any link to your identity.') }}
  • {{ _('Picker / informal-worker registration:') }} {{ _('the details you provide to join the collection ecosystem.') }}

b) {{ _('GPS location') }}

  • {{ _('When you file a report or use the emergency cleanup request, the portal asks your browser for GPS coordinates. These are used to route field crews, verify that a report genuinely refers to the stated location, and to power the ward weather module.') }}
  • {{ _('Location data is not continuously tracked — it is captured only at the moment you submit.') }}

c) {{ _('Photos') }}

  • {{ _('You may optionally attach photos as evidence (e.g., an overflowing bin). Before storage, photos are automatically stripped of embedded metadata (EXIF/GPS) to protect your privacy.') }}
  • {{ _('Cleared-bin "after" photos are used by field staff to verify that a complaint has been resolved.') }}

d) {{ _('Payments (PAYT)') }}

  • {{ _('Pay-As-You-Throw invoices record the weight of non-recyclable waste billed, the invoice amount, payment method, transaction reference and status.') }}
  • {{ _('Payments are processed by Razorpay on their secure checkout page. Card and UPI details go directly to Razorpay and are never received or stored by us; we receive only the order/payment references needed to confirm and reconcile the payment, and to process refunds.') }}

e) {{ _('Cookies and analytics') }}

  • {{ _('Essential cookies keep you signed in, protect forms against abuse, and support the offline-report queue. These are always active and do not require consent.') }}
  • {{ _('Optional anonymous analytics (Google Analytics-style aggregate usage statistics) are only enabled after you click Accept on the consent banner. No advertising or cross-site tracking is used.') }}

f) {{ _('Operational data (not personal)') }}

  • {{ _('Bin telemetry:') }} {{ _('fill level, temperature, methane and battery readings sent by IoT sensors on public bins. These relate to equipment, not to individuals.') }}
  • {{ _('Audit logs:') }} {{ _('records of administrative actions (who did what and when) for security and accountability.') }}
