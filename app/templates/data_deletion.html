What happens after you file

  1. We verify the email address or mobile number you give us against our records.
  2. We erase your name, email, phone number, one-time codes and login credentials. Your account can no longer sign in.
  3. We retain the complaint, billing and audit history the Gram Panchayat is legally required to keep — with your identity removed from it.
  4. We reply with the outcome, or tell you plainly why the request could not be completed.

File a request
