{# ── H3: 6.1 Collection schedules ── #}

6.1 {{ _('Collection schedules') }}

Q: How do I check my collection schedule?
A: Go to the schedule page, select your ward, and view today\'s pickup timetable. No login needed.
Q: Which days are collection days?
A: Each ward has a published weekly timetable. Wet waste is collected on most days; dry recyclables on specific days; bulk items on scheduled quarterly drives. See the full schedule for your ward.
Q: What if my scheduled collection is missed?
A: Use the report page to file a missed-pickup complaint with your address and a photo. You get a tracking link by SMS.
{# ── H3: 6.2 Reporting problems ── #}

6.2 {{ _('Reporting problems') }}

Q: How do I report a missed service?
A: File a complaint on the report page with your address, GPS location, and photo. You receive a tracking link via SMS to follow progress.
Q: Can I track my complaint?
A: Yes. You get a tracking link via SMS when you file. Registered users can also view all their complaints on the citizen dashboard.
Q: How long does a complaint take to resolve?
A: The panchayat targets resolution within 18 hours on average, down from 72 hours before the system was introduced. You can see the current average on the statistics section above.
{# ── H3: 6.3 Green Points and rewards ── #}

6.3 {{ _('Green Points and rewards') }}

Q: What are Green Points?
A: Residents earn 15 points per valid complaint report and additional points for waste segregation declarations. Points can be redeemed for vouchers and tax discounts under the Swachh Bharat Mission framework.
Q: How do I earn Green Points?
A: Register on the portal, file valid complaints, and submit waste segregation declarations. Your points balance is visible on your citizen dashboard.
Q: Can I see the leaderboard?
A: Yes — the impact dashboard shows top-scoring residents and ward-level engagement, encouraging healthy competition and community participation.
{# ── H3: 6.4 Wards and coverage ── #}

6.4 {{ _('Wards and coverage') }}

Q: Which wards are covered?
A: All five wards are covered: MVGR College Area, Junction, RTC Colony, Ramalayam Street, and Sai Nagar. Each has its own collection schedule and ward dashboard.
Q: Is there a fee to use this service?
A: No. The portal is free for all residents. Pay-as-you-throw billing applies only to bulk waste generators as part of the segregation incentives programme.
Q: How do I check bin status in my area?
A: Visit the transparency page for live bin fill levels and complaint resolution rates by ward.
