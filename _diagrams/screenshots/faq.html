      ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

      Go to the schedules page and select your ward. Timetables update daily before 7 AM — no login needed. You can check today's schedule for any of the 5 wards in Chintalavalasa: MVGR College Area, Chintalavalasa Junction, RTC Colony, Ramalayam Street, and Sai Nagar. The schedule shows the expected arrival window for the sanitation crew in your street.

      Use the report page to file a complaint with your address and photo. No account needed — you get a tracking link via SMS. Average resolution: under 24 hours. GPS coordinates are captured automatically so the crew can locate the issue quickly.

      Keep dry recyclables (paper, plastic, metal) separate from wet food waste. Two bags, two categories — it helps recycle 40% more material. The portal also lets you declare your daily segregation to earn Green Points.

      Yes. You get a tracking link via SMS. Register to see real-time status on your dashboard: filed → assigned → resolved. Average resolution time across all 5 wards is under 24 hours.

      Earn 15 points per report filed. Points can be redeemed for vouchers and tax discounts under the Swachh Bharat Mission incentive framework. You also earn points for declaring daily waste segregation.

      All 5 wards: MVGR College Area, Chintalavalasa Junction, RTC Colony, Ramalayam Street, and Sai Nagar. Each has a live transparency dashboard. The service serves approximately 12,000 residents across Vizianagaram District.

      Yes, it is free. All core services — schedules, reporting, tracking — require no payment or login. PAYT billing applies only to bulk waste generators who exceed household norms. The portal is funded by the Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen) Phase II.

      Visit the transparency page to see live bin fill levels, resolution rates, and segregation data for your ward. The dashboard shows real-time data from smart bins and community records.
